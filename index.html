Objective

Understand why shelf availability was inconsistent at a national fast-fashion retailer, and design a practical fix — without assuming the problem was simply too few staff.

Approach

  • Reframed the root cause as an information gap, not a resourcing shortfall.
  • Mapped the current (As-Is) replenishment workflow and designed a To-Be process.
  • Defined requirements to prioritise pick tasks by sales velocity using existing handheld terminals.
  • Wrote user stories with acceptance criteria for the system-guided solution.

Tools & Methods

Root-cause analysis · Process mapping (As-Is / To-Be) · Requirements engineering · User-story mapping & acceptance criteria · Stakeholder analysis.

Outcome

  • Targeted cutting replenishment cycle times by more than half.
  • Aimed to improve peak-hour shelf availability from 92% to 98%.
  • Reduced stockroom overstock through smarter, data-backed reordering.

Presented as a structured case study; figures are project targets based on the analysis.

Objective

Determine whether continued and increased social-media marketing investment is justified by real evidence of how platforms influence purchase behaviour.

Approach

  • Conducted 14 semi-structured interviews with active UK social-media users aged 19–39.
  • Analysed transcripts using thematic analysis in NVivo.
  • Translated findings into a stakeholder-ready business case with options appraisal.

Key Findings

  • Trend-driven, authentic content drives engagement more than polished, overtly promotional posts.
  • Authenticity and trust in the source are preconditions for purchase intent.
  • Urgency and scarcity tactics work short-term but risk post-purchase regret.
  • Over-personalised, repetitive advertising causes frustration beyond a "personalisation ceiling".

Recommendation

Prioritise authentic, trend-responsive content; cap ad frequency and monitor sentiment; and track platform-specific engagement separately rather than as a single channel.

Tools & Methods

Qualitative interviews · Thematic analysis · NVivo · Business-case development · Options appraisal · Stakeholder mapping.
