Redesigning In-Store Replenishment at a National Fast-Fashion Retailer

Process Improvement · Business Analysis Case Study

A process-improvement project tackling inconsistent shelf availability. Rather than treating it as a staffing problem, I framed the root cause as an information gap — and proposed a data-led, system-guided replenishment method that reuses existing handheld terminals to prioritise pick tasks by sales velocity and surface exact stockroom bin locations.

  • Aimed to cut replenishment cycle times by more than half.
  • Targeted an improvement in peak-hour shelf availability from 92% to 98%.
  • Reduced stockroom overstock through smarter, data-backed reordering.
  • Diagnosed the root cause, mapped the As-Is and designed the To-Be process, and wrote user stories with acceptance criteria.

Stakeholder Analysis · Elicitation Techniques · Process Modeling · Requirements Engineering · User Story Mapping & Acceptance Criteria.

SMM Investment Analysis — Optimising Social Media Marketing Spend

Business Case · Primary Qualitative Research

A stakeholder-ready business case — translated from my dissertation research — evaluating whether continued and increased social-media marketing investment (Instagram, TikTok, Facebook) is justified by real evidence of how these platforms drive purchase behaviour. Grounded in 14 semi-structured interviews with active UK users aged 19–39, analysed via thematic analysis in NVivo.

  • Trend-driven, authentic content drives engagement more than polished, overtly promotional posts.
  • Authenticity and trust in the source are preconditions for purchase intent.
  • Urgency and scarcity tactics work short-term but risk longer-term post-purchase regret.
  • Over-personalised, repetitive advertising causes frustration beyond a "personalisation ceiling."

Recommendation: prioritise authentic, trend-responsive content; cap ad frequency and monitor sentiment; and track platform-specific engagement separately rather than as a single channel.

Business Case Development · Qualitative Research · Thematic Analysis (NVivo) · Options Appraisal · Stakeholder Mapping.
