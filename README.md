## 👋 My Story

My journey into business analysis began in **Kathmandu**, where a degree in Information
Technology taught me to see organisations as systems — databases, processes, and the
people who depend on them. I was less interested in how technology works than in **how it
creates value**.

That curiosity led me to **Cloud Factory**, where I spent three years as a Data Specialist
supporting high-growth retail-tech clients like Ibotta and Pathr.ai. Inside Agile teams, I
learned the craft that still defines me: turning ambiguous client needs into precise,
prioritised requirements — and holding a standard of quality (98% accuracy) even in the
most complex workflows. Alongside that, I created content and presented on camera at
**Falcon Tech**, a digital marketing agency, which taught me how people actually make
decisions and how to communicate an idea clearly enough that a stranger will act on it.

Wanting to connect technology with strategy, I moved to the **UK** for an MSc in
International Business Management at Wrexham University. My dissertation — later reworked
into a stakeholder-ready business case — grounded marketing decisions in real qualitative
research, and confirmed what I value most: **turning evidence into decisions people can
act on**.

Today I bring all of that together. At **Primark**, I analyse sales and inventory data to
reshape operational processes (reducing overstock by 90%+ and helping my department reach
a Top 5 national ranking), and as a volunteer language mentor I help young learners find
their own relevance in what they study. Across every role, one thread holds: I listen
closely, map what's really happening, and design the clearest path to a better outcome.

> I'm a **Business Analyst** because I believe the most valuable thing you can do for a
> business is to understand it — genuinely — and then help it change.

## 🚀 Personal Projects

### 1. Redesigning In-Store Replenishment at a National Fast-Fashion Retailer
*Process Improvement · Business Analysis Case Study*

Tackled inconsistent shelf availability by reframing the root cause as an **information
gap** rather than a staffing shortfall. Proposed a data-led, system-guided replenishment
method that reuses existing handheld terminals to prioritise pick tasks by sales velocity
and surface exact stockroom bin locations.

- ⏱️ Cuts replenishment cycle times by **more than half**
- 📈 Improves peak-hour shelf availability from **92% → 98%**
- 📦 Reduces stockroom overstock through smarter, data-backed reordering

`Stakeholder Analysis` · `Elicitation Techniques` · `Process Modeling` · `Requirements Engineering` · `User Story Mapping & Acceptance Criteria`

### 2. SMM Investment Analysis — Optimising Social Media Marketing Spend
*Business Case · Primary Qualitative Research*

A stakeholder-ready business case (translated from my MSc dissertation) evaluating whether
increased social-media marketing investment is justified by real evidence of purchase
behaviour. Grounded in **14 semi-structured interviews** with active UK users aged 19–39,
analysed through thematic analysis in NVivo.

- Identified four decision-driving themes: trend-driven content, authenticity & trust,
  urgency/scarcity tactics, and over-personalisation fatigue
- Recommended a refined strategy: prioritise authentic content, cap ad frequency, and
  track engagement per platform rather than as a single channel

`Business Case Development` · `Qualitative Research` · `Thematic Analysis (NVivo)` · `Options Appraisal` · `Stakeholder Mapping`

### 3. Healthcare Patient Information Management
*Case Study*

Designed an end-to-end digital solution to centralise fragmented patient records, improving
clinical workflow efficiency and data compliance. Applied Agile methodology to build a
product backlog, documented functional user stories for secure role-based access, and
mapped the patient journey to reduce administrative processing times and data-entry errors.

`Agile` · `Product Backlog` · `User Stories` · `Process Mapping`
